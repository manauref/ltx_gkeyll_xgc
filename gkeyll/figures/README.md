- lipass_103795_03-base: base passivated Li shot sim, w/ D=30 m^2/s.

- int_moms: integrated moments (normalized to the final value).
- final_den_temp_z1mid: final density and temperature at OMP.
- init_den_temp_z1mid: initial density and temperature at OMP.
- src_den_temp_z1mid: source density source rate and temperatures.
- grid_RZ: grid on the R-Z plane.
- init_elc_den_z1mid: initial electron density at OMP.
- src_Ndot_Power: source particle source rate and power.
- init_elc_den_RZ: initial electron density on the R-Z plane.
- final_elc_den_RZ: final electron density on the R-Z plane.
- final_elc_upar_RZ: final electron u_parallel on the R-Z plane.
- final_elc_temp_RZ: final electron temperature on the R-Z plane.
- final_elc_tpar_RZ: final electron T_parallel on the R-Z plane.
- final_elc_tperp_RZ: final electron T_perp on the R-Z plane.
- final_ion_den_RZ: final ion density on the R-Z plane.
- final_ion_upar_RZ: final ion u_parallel on the R-Z plane.
- final_ion_temp_RZ: final ion temperature on the R-Z plane.
- final_ion_tpar_RZ: final ion T_parallel on the R-Z plane.
- final_ion_tperp_RZ: final ion T_perp on the R-Z plane.
- final_nu_ee_z1mid: final e-e collision frequency at the OMP.
- final_nu_ie_z1mid: final i-e collision frequency at the OMP.
- final_nu_ii_z1mid: final i-i collision frequency at the OMP.
- final_nu_eStar_z1mid: final electron collisionality at the OMP.
- final_nu_iStar_z1mid: final ion collisionality at the OMP. 
- liComp863mgPass_final_den_temp_z1mid: final density and temperature at OMP for Li 863 mg and passivated shots.
